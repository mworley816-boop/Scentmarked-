## Request template

Subject: Request for authorized fragrance product data access — Scentmarked

Hello My Perfumes team,

I operate Scentmarked, a fragrance discovery and comparison website. We would like permission to use an official product data feed or to retrieve a limited number of public Arabiyat and Arabiyat Prestige product pages for factual fragrance information such as product names, concentrations, and top, heart and base notes.

Our proposed use would link to your original product pages, attribute the source, honor agreed request limits, and require a human review before adding or updating a catalog entry. We would not copy product photography or substantial marketing descriptions without separate permission.

Do you offer an official product feed or API? If not, would you authorize a limited automated retrieval process? Please let us know any access, caching, attribution, robots, or commercial-use conditions.

Thank you,
Scentmarked
